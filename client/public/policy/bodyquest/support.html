Frequently asked

Is BodyQuest medical advice?

No. It is health education. Nothing in it - including anything in the Shop - is medical advice. Talk to a clinician before changing your diet, starting exercise, or taking a supplement.

Why does iOS ask about tracking?

BodyQuest shows ads through Google AdMob. The prompt asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier for ad relevance. Decline it and everything still works.

Do you earn money from the Shop?

Yes. Shop entries link to Amazon and iHerb with affiliate tags, so a purchase may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. It does not change what is shown.

Does the app track my health measurements?

No. BodyQuest is educational and does not record vitals, and it integrates with neither Apple Health nor Google Fit.

How do I remove ads?

The Premium upgrade removes them. The free app is otherwise fully usable.

How do I delete my account?

Email us and we will action it within 30 days.
