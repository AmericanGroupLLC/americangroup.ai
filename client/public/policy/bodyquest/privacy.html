Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-08-28 · Applies to BodyQuest for iOS and Android.

BodyQuest is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

2. What the app does not collect

  • Health measurements - BodyQuest is educational and does not record vitals
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos or microphone
  • Data from Apple Health or Google Fit - the app integrates with neither

3. Location

Not requested and not used.

4. Third parties

Authentication and sync run on Supabase. Ads are served by Google AdMob under its own privacy policy. Shop links open Amazon and iHerb, which apply their own policies once you leave the app. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google.

5. Children

Not directed at children under 13. Because the app serves ads and links to commerce, it is not suitable for a children's category.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

Email us to have your account and its progress deleted; we action it within 30 days. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

Progress persists while your account exists. All traffic uses HTTPS.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
