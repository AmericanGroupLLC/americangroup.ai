Ratio Tune — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

American Group LLC ("we", "our", "us") builds Ratio Tune. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, why it uses it, and how your information is handled. It reflects the app's actual behavior and permissions.

Ratio Tune has no user accounts. We do not request your name, email address, phone number, or payment information. There is no sign-up or login.

1. What the App Collects

Approximate Location (Optional)

Ratio Tune requests Android's ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION permission only to display nearby radio stations.

  • If granted, your approximate (city-level) location is sent to the Radio Browser API to retrieve nearby stations.
  • Your location is used only for that request.
  • We do not store your location.
  • We do not keep location history.
  • We do not request precise GPS location.
  • If permission is denied, only the "Near You" feature is unavailable.

Notifications (Optional)

On Android 13 and later, the app requests the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission the first time audio playback starts.

This permission is used only for media playback notifications and lock-screen controls. No promotional or marketing notifications are sent.

Saved Favorites

Stations marked as favorites are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted to us.

Cached Artwork

Station logos are cached locally to improve loading speed and reduce data usage.

Analytics, Crash Reporting, and Advertising SDKs

Ratio Tune contains no analytics, crash reporting, or attribution SDKs. The app does display advertising through Google AdMob.

What We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not directly collect or maintain user accounts or personal profiles containing:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Listening history or search history
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, or health data

We do not sell, rent, or profile user data.

Google Play may collect anonymous crash and performance information for the Play Console. This data is collected by Google, not by the Ratio Tune application.

2. Advertising

Ratio Tune displays banner and interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

To deliver, personalize where applicable, and measure advertising, Google may collect and process information such as your Android Advertising ID, IP address, general device information, and interactions with advertisements.

This information may be processed by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

Your saved favorite stations and locally stored listening-related information are not shared by Ratio Tune with advertisers.

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through your Android device settings. The exact location of this setting may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

3. Third-Party Services

Radio Browser API

Ratio Tune communicates directly with api.radio-browser.info to retrieve radio station information.

The following may be sent:

  • Search queries
  • Selected filters
  • Approximate location (only when permission is granted)
  • Your IP address (standard internet communication)

This service operates under its own privacy policy.

Radio Streaming Servers

When you play a station, your device connects directly to that station's streaming server.

Those operators may receive your IP address. Their privacy practices are outside our control.

Some stations still stream audio over HTTP instead of HTTPS. These streams are not encrypted in transit. Requests to the Radio Browser API use HTTPS.

Station Artwork

Station logos are downloaded from URLs supplied by the Radio Browser directory. Loading these images exposes your IP address to the image host.

4. Children's Privacy

Ratio Tune is not intended for children under the age of 13. The app streams third-party radio and podcast content that we do not create or moderate. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention

American Group LLC does not operate servers that store the app's locally saved favorites or cached artwork.

App data remains on your device unless it is transmitted to a third-party service as described in this Privacy Policy, such as requests to the Radio Browser API or connections to radio streaming and artwork servers.

  • Uninstalling the app removes favorites and cached artwork stored by the app on your device.
  • You may also clear app data through Android Settings.
  • Permissions can be revoked at any time through Android Settings.

6. Security

Requests to the Radio Browser API use HTTPS. Since we do not maintain user accounts or store user credentials on our servers, there are no user account credentials maintained by American Group LLC.

Please note that some radio streams may use unencrypted HTTP. Those streams are not encrypted in transit.

If this policy changes, the Effective Date will be updated and the revised version will be published at this page.
