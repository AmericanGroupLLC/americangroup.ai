Questions, bug reports and feature requests for Ratio Tune all go to the same place, and a person reads them.

Contact us

Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Ratio Tune has no sign-in and no profile, and every feature works on first launch.

Why does it ask for my location?

Only if you open Discover. An approximate position is sent to the Radio Browser directory once to list stations near you. It is not stored and is not used for anything else - declining leaves the rest of the app working.

A station will not play, or has gone.

Streams are published by the broadcasters themselves and listed in the open Radio Browser directory. If one is offline or has changed format, that is at their end; try another station, and the directory usually catches up.

Where are my favourites kept?

On your device only. They are not uploaded, so they do not follow you to another phone and are cleared if you delete the app.

What happened to Live Events?

There is no event data source behind it yet, so rather than list invented concerts the feature is not shipped. It will return when it has real data.
