Support

Questions and bug reports for TripNest go to one place. Note that we cannot change, cancel or refund a booking - that belongs to the partner you booked with.

Contact us

Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. TripNest has no sign-in at all. Trips and saved options live on your device.

I booked and need to change or cancel it.

Contact the partner you booked with. TripNest hands off to their site and never holds your booking or payment, so we cannot alter it.

A price changed when I reached the partner's site.

Fares and rates move constantly and partners hold the authoritative price. Always confirm the total on their checkout before paying.

Why does the app want my location?

Only to detect your origin so deals are relevant. Decline it and type an origin instead.

Do you earn money from bookings?

Yes - outbound partner links are affiliate links and may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes the order of results, and that is disclosed in-app.

Where did my trips go?

They are stored on your device, so clearing app data or uninstalling removes them.

Report a privacy concern or request deletion

See the Privacy Policy, or email contact@americangroup.ai with the subject “Data request”.
