Privacy Policy — TripNest

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

American Group LLC ("American Group LLC", "we", "us", or "our") operates the TripNest mobile application ("TripNest" or the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use TripNest.

Important: TripNest is currently an initial release. Live travel pricing and booking functionality are coming soon.

1. Information We Collect

TripNest is designed to minimize the information it collects. In the current version, TripNest does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the application.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Some information you enter or create while using TripNest may be stored locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Traveller name
  • Home city
  • Currency preference
  • Payment country preference
  • Fare-alert preference
  • Saved travel options
  • Trip information
  • Recent search information
  • App preferences such as theme settings

This information is stored locally by the application and is not transmitted to American Group LLC servers in the current release. TripNest does not operate a server-side account system for this information.

2. Information TripNest Does Not Collect

In the current release, TripNest does not itself collect:

  • Account passwords or authentication credentials
  • Email addresses for account creation
  • Payment card or bank account information
  • Precise location information
  • Approximate location through device location permissions
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Files or documents
  • Calendar information
  • Health information

TripNest does not request Android location permissions in the current release.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

TripNest displays advertisements using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

AdMob may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, diagnostics, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, and information about advertising interactions. The information collected and the purposes for which it is processed may vary depending on Google's advertising and consent settings.

TripNest does not directly receive or store the advertising data collected by Google AdMob for its own account system.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Advertising ID and Ad Choices

Google AdMob may use the Android Advertising ID and other advertising-related identifiers for purposes such as serving, measuring, and improving advertisements.

Depending on your device and region, you may be able to reset, restrict, or otherwise manage advertising personalization through your Android device's privacy and advertising settings.

TripNest also uses Google's consent management technology where required to obtain advertising consent before requesting ads.

5. Android Permissions

TripNest uses permissions and Android services necessary for its operation and advertising functionality.

These may include:

  • Internet/network access — required for network communication and advertising.
  • Network state information — used to determine network availability.
  • Advertising-related Android services — used by Google advertising services.

TripNest does not request location permissions in the current release.

6. How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to provide application features such as preferences, saved information, trip planning, and other local functionality.

Information processed by Google AdMob may be used for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and related advertising purposes according to Google's policies and applicable consent requirements.

7. Information Sharing

American Group LLC does not sell your locally stored TripNest information.

In the current release, the main third-party service integrated into TripNest for data processing is Google AdMob for advertising.

If you follow a link to a third-party travel provider or other external website in a future version of TripNest, that website will operate under its own privacy policy and terms.

8. Travel Booking and Live Pricing

TripNest's current release does not provide live travel pricing or live booking functionality. These features are planned for future releases.

When live inventory or booking services are introduced, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play Data Safety information will be updated to describe any additional data transmitted to travel providers or TripNest services.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by TripNest remains on your device until you delete it using the available in-app deletion controls, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

TripNest does not maintain a server-side copy of this local information in the current release.

Information processed by Google AdMob is retained and handled according to Google's applicable policies and retention practices.

10. Security

TripNest is designed to minimize the amount of personal information it handles. The application does not store account passwords or payment credentials.

Network communication used by the application is configured to use secure connections where network communication is required.

11. Children's Privacy

TripNest is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

TripNest is not designed as a child-directed application. Advertising and consent behavior are configured according to applicable Google policies and user consent requirements.

12. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers such as Google may process information in countries other than the country in which you live.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards and consent mechanisms will be used for international data processing.

13. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

You can also:

  • Delete locally stored TripNest information using the available in-app controls.
  • Uninstall TripNest to remove its locally stored application data.
  • Manage advertising and privacy choices through supported Android and Google settings.
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions or requests.

14. Third-Party Services

TripNest uses third-party services where necessary to provide application functionality. The current release includes Google AdMob for advertising.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review those policies before using third-party services.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to TripNest, applicable laws, or third-party services.

When we make material changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.
