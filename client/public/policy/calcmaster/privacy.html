CalcMaster collects only the minimum information required to provide application functionality and display advertisements. The app does not require user registration and does not collect personal profile information.

Location permission is optional and is used only to determine the user's country for regional calculations and currency conversion. Users may deny this permission and continue using the application.

Advertising

CalcMaster uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may collect device identifiers such as the Advertising ID according to Google's Privacy Policy. American Group LLC does not sell users' personal information.

Authentication

The current version does not provide login, registration, Google Sign-In, or social authentication.
