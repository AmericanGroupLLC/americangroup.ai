Frequent questions

Is CalcMaster really free? Yes. The free tier includes every calculator, every conversion, and every tool. CalcMaster Pro removes the small banner ad and adds advanced insights.

Why does the GPS tool ask for location? Only when you tap "Use my location" inside the GPS Coordinates tool. The coordinates are shown to you and never transmitted off-device.

How accurate are the currency rates? Rates come from the European Central Bank via api.frankfurter.dev. They update once per business day. Pro subscribers get a more frequent refresh interval.

How do I cancel CalcMaster Pro?

  • iOS: Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → CalcMaster → Cancel.
  • Android: Play Store → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CalcMaster → Cancel.

Does CalcMaster work offline? Yes. Currency rates cache locally and refresh when you reconnect; everything else works without a network.

What languages do you support? 12 today: English, Español, 中文, हिन्दी, Français, العربية, Deutsch, 日本語, Português, Italiano, 한국어, Русский. The app picks your device language automatically.
