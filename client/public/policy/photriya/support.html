Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Photriya has no login and no account creation. Every feature works on first launch.

Why does the app want camera access?

The Capture tab reads colours from the live camera feed. Frames are processed on your device and not retained. Decline it and the rest of the app still works.

Are my photos uploaded?

Only the ones you explicitly submit to the AI Playground, and only when you start that action. Colour capture, the library, the histogram and EXIF tools all run on-device.

The EXIF Viewer shows a GPS location.

That coordinate is stored in your photo file by the camera that took it. Photriya reads it from your own file and never transmits it.

How do I export a palette?

Open Library, select a colour or palette, and choose Export - PNG, SVG, ASE and JSON are supported.

How do I change the language?

Settings - Language. Ten are available and the change applies immediately.
