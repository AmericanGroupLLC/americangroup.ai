Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-10-12 · Applies to Photriya for iOS and Android.

Photriya is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

2. What the app does not collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your contacts, microphone, or device location
  • Your photo library as a whole - only the photos you pick
  • Any identifier used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites - ads are non-tracking (see section 4)

3. Location

Read only from a photo's existing EXIF data when you open the EXIF Viewer. The app does not request device location.

4. Third parties

AI analysis and generation run on a third-party model provider, which necessarily receives the photo you submit in order to return a result. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Google also receives the advertising and diagnostic data described below; no other third party receives your data.

Advertising. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner on the main screens and, occasionally, a full-screen ad between screens (at most one every few minutes). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under “Privacy choices” in the app. Ads are non-tracking: we do not link ad data to your identity or use it to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. To serve and measure ads, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier and IP address, as described in Google’s privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

Crash reporting and analytics. To find and fix problems, the app sends crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics (the error, the device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier), and anonymous usage statistics and performance measurements through Google Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring. This data is not linked to your name or account, we do not use it for advertising, and Google processes it under Firebase’s privacy terms.

5. Children

Photriya is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

There is no account, so nothing exists server-side to delete. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes your entire local library, history and settings. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

Your library and history stay on your device until you delete them or clear app data. Photos submitted for AI processing are transmitted over HTTPS and are not retained by us after the result is returned.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
