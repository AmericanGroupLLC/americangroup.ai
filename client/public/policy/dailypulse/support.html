Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Sign-in exists but is not required to read any feed.

Why does the app want my location?

To pick weather, air quality and local news. Decline it and set a city by name instead - everything still works.

Does the assistant send my messages to an AI company?

No. This build ships without an OpenAI key, so the assistant runs on a rule-based engine on your device.

Is the stock watchlist connected to my brokerage?

No. It holds ticker symbols only. The app has no brokerage link and takes no financial credentials.

How is Good News decided?

Articles are scored by sentiment analysis and the feed shows the positively-scored ones. It is automated, so it is imperfect.

How do I change the language?

Settings - Language. Nine are available and the change applies immediately.
