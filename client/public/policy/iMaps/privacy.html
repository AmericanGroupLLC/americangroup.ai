iMaps is a community-powered navigation app. It shows a map, plans driving routes, lets people report road incidents, aggregates public hazard data near you, and lists public-safety radio streams. This policy describes how the application handles information and how third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads, may process information when you use the app.

The short version. iMaps works without an account and without requiring a name, phone number, or email for normal guest use. It does not track your location in the background, does not read your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files, and does not sell personal information. The app does use your location while you actively use location-based features, and sends relevant coordinates to mapping, routing, and public-safety services.

iMaps also displays advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Depending on device settings, Google's advertising services may process the Android Advertising ID and other device or advertising-related signals for purposes such as serving, measuring, and improving advertising.

2. What we do not collect

iMaps itself does not intentionally access or transmit the following:

  • Your name, phone number, postal address, or date of birth as part of ordinary guest use.
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS messages, or your calendar.
  • Camera, microphone, photos, media, or files on your device.
  • Biometric data, health or fitness data, or clipboard contents.
  • IMEI, MAC address, or phone number.
  • Installed-app inventory or browsing history outside the app.
  • Payment card numbers or billing details. These are handled by Google Play and are not provided to iMaps.
  • A continuous history of your movements. iMaps does not perform background location tracking.

Advertising exception: Google Mobile Ads is integrated into the app. Google may process the Android Advertising ID and other device or advertising-related signals as described in the Advertising section below.

3. Location

Location is a core feature of iMaps and is used while you actively use location-based features.

What it is used for

  • Showing your position on the map and centring the view.
  • Planning driving routes and providing navigation information.
  • Finding nearby community incident reports.
  • Fetching public hazard data around you, including earthquakes, weather alerts, wildfires, disasters and municipal reports.
  • Finding nearby EV chargers, fuel stations, parking, transit stops, speed limits and elevation.
  • Showing local weather and selecting relevant public-safety radio streams.
  • Selecting regionally relevant partner offers.

How location is shared

To provide these features, relevant coordinates may be sent from your device to third-party services listed in section 13. Requests are made over HTTPS. We do not create a continuous location history or location profile.

Location is sensitive information. Several public data services may receive coordinates to answer "what is near me?" requests. These include mapping, routing, weather, earthquake, wildfire, air-quality, public-safety and municipal services. Those services have their own privacy policies and data practices.

Declining location

Location permission is requested at runtime when required. You may refuse or revoke it at any time. The app can continue to run without location, although features that depend on your position may be limited or unavailable.

4. Background location

iMaps does not collect location in the background. The app does not request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION and does not use background location tracking. Location access is limited to active use of the app.

5. Incident reports

Reporting is optional and does not require an account. When you submit a report, it is stored locally and transmitted to the iMaps backend so that other users can see the report.

A report may contain the incident type, coordinates, an optional description, optional street name, timestamp, and a random device identifier.

Reports are public. A report you submit may be visible to other iMaps users. Do not enter names, licence plates, phone numbers, addresses, or other personal information into the description field.

6. Accounts and sign-in

iMaps is designed to be usable without an account. If account functionality is enabled in a particular release, signing in is optional.

Where available, authentication may be provided through Supabase and supported identity providers. Information processed during sign-in can include your email address and, where an identity provider supplies it, your name.

Signing out clears the active session from the device.

7. Notifications

Notifications are generated locally by iMaps. The application does not use a remote push-notification service for its local incident alerts.

When applicable, iMaps may display a local Android notification about a nearby incident. You can control notifications through the app settings and Android notification permissions.

10. Crash reporting

iMaps does not integrate a dedicated third-party crash-reporting SDK. The application does not intentionally transmit crash logs, stack traces, or performance traces to its own backend for crash reporting.

Google Play and Android may independently provide developer diagnostics or crash information according to Google's services and your device/account settings. Such processing is governed by Google's applicable policies.

11. Advertising

iMaps displays advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

The current release uses AdMob for:

  • Banner advertisements, displayed within the application interface.
  • Interstitial advertisements, displayed at selected natural breaks in the application experience.

The application does not use rewarded advertisements and does not provide a "Watch Ad" or similar rewarded-ad button.

Information used for advertising

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or access certain information from the device in order to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve advertising. Depending on Google's services, device configuration, and applicable consent or privacy settings, this can include:

  • Android Advertising ID or other advertising identifiers.
  • Device and network information.
  • IP address or approximate network-derived location.
  • Ad interaction and performance information.
  • Information required to detect invalid traffic and fraud.

The precise information processed by Google may vary depending on the advertising request, device configuration, regional requirements, and Google's current services.

Important: iMaps does not sell your personal information to advertisers. Advertisements are provided through Google Mobile Ads, and Google may process information under its own privacy policies and applicable advertising technologies.

Advertising identifier controls

Android provides controls that allow you to reset, delete, or restrict use of the Advertising ID depending on your Android version and device manufacturer. You can review these settings under your device's Privacy or Ads settings.

You can learn more about Google's handling of information from apps that use Google services here: Google — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

Where required by applicable law, advertising-related processing and consent choices are handled in accordance with Google's and the app's applicable consent mechanisms.

12. Purchases

iMaps may offer optional premium subscriptions. Payment processing is performed by Google Play Billing.

The app does not receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or Google account password.

To verify a purchase, the app may send the platform name, product identifier, and signed purchase information to the iMaps backend. The resulting entitlement is stored on the device.

16. External links

Some actions open a third-party website or application outside iMaps.

  • Partner offers — may open a partner website using a referral tag.
  • Radio streams — may open OpenMHz, Broadcastify, LiveATC, or another external media destination.
  • Suggestion cards — may open partner websites.

Once you leave iMaps, the destination site's privacy policy and cookie practices apply. iMaps does not control those external services.

17. Cookies and advertising technologies

The native Android application does not intentionally use browser cookies for its own application storage.

However, third-party advertising services such as Google Mobile Ads may use advertising technologies, identifiers, device signals, or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements.

This privacy policy page is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, according to its own privacy policy.

For more information about Google's advertising technologies and how Google processes information from apps using its services, see: Google Privacy & Terms .

18. Retention

  • On your device — preferences and identifiers remain until you clear the application's data or uninstall it.
  • Incident reports — retained on the backend as needed to operate and moderate the community reporting service.
  • Analytics events — retained as needed for product measurement and service operation.
  • Account data — if you sign in, account information is retained according to the applicable authentication service and our operational requirements.
  • Advertising data — information processed by Google Mobile Ads is retained according to Google's policies, systems, and applicable legal requirements.

19. Your rights and choices

Controls available immediately

  • Revoke location at any time through Android app permissions.
  • Turn off alerts through the app's settings or Android notification controls.
  • Stay a guest and avoid providing account information.
  • Reset local identifiers by clearing application data or uninstalling the application.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID through the advertising or privacy settings provided by your Android device.

Advertising choices

Android provides controls for resetting or deleting the Advertising ID and, depending on the Android version and device manufacturer, controls related to personalized advertising.

Google's advertising privacy information is available at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Legal rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws, including rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal information.

We do not sell personal information. We also do not intentionally provide your personal information to third parties for their own independent direct-marketing purposes.

To submit a privacy request, contact: contact@americangroup.ai .

Where processing depends on an Android permission or other user-controlled setting, you may withdraw or modify that permission through your device settings.

International transfers

The services described in this policy may operate in multiple countries, including the United States. Using iMaps may therefore involve transferring information to services located outside your country of residence.

20. Children

iMaps is a driving and navigation application intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the application, contact us at contact@americangroup.ai .

21. Security

  • Network traffic between the app and supported services uses HTTPS.
  • Android application storage is used for local preferences and identifiers.
  • Purchases are verified through the application's purchase-verification process before premium entitlement is granted.
  • Advertising processing is performed through Google's Mobile Ads infrastructure and is subject to Google's security and privacy practices.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
