Frequently asked

Why does iMaps need my location?

To build a route from where you are, and to decide which hazard alerts fall inside the two-mile radius. Without it the map cannot centre and the alert feed cannot filter.

Do I need an account?

No. iMaps has no sign-in and no profile.

Where do the alerts come from?

Five public feeds: USGS for earthquakes, NOAA/NWS for severe weather, NASA FIRMS for wildfires, GDACS for global disasters and Open311 for municipal reports.

A radio stream will not play. What now?

Public safety streams are operated by third parties and go offline without notice. Try another stream; if every stream fails, check whether your network blocks streaming audio.

An incident report is wrong.

Tap the marker and dismiss it. Reports also lose severity over time and disappear once they expire.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. Fifteen are available and the change applies immediately.
