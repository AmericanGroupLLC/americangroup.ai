You may not have an account at all. iMaps does not require one — the app works fully as a guest, and most people never sign in. You only have an account if you explicitly signed in from Settings → Account → Sign in using an email address, Google, or Apple. If you have never signed in, there is no account to delete; simply uninstalling the app removes everything iMaps stored on your device.

How to request deletion

Account deletion is handled by email request. There is no charge, and you do not need the app installed to make the request.

Send your deletion request to:

Email contact@americangroup.ai

Opens your mail app with the request pre-filled

  1. Email contact@americangroup.ai with the subject line “iMaps account deletion request”.
  2. Include the email address associated with your account — the address you registered with, or the one attached to the Google or Apple account you used to sign in — and tell us which sign-in method you used.
  3. Send it from that same email address where possible. This is how we verify you own the account. If you cannot, we will ask you to confirm ownership another way before deleting anything.
  4. We confirm. You will receive an acknowledgement, and a further confirmation once the deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

DataOutcome

You can do this yourself at any time, with or without a deletion request.

What is not deleted, and why

Community incident reports cannot be linked back to your account. When you report a pothole, an accident or a hazard, iMaps attaches a randomly generated identifier that belongs to your app installation — not to your account. Your account and your reports are never joined together, by design, so that reporting stays anonymous. This also means that deleting your account does not and cannot identify which reports were yours.

  • Incident reports remain part of the shared community map. They contain no name, no email address and no account reference. Reports also disappear from the app's active view automatically after two hours, and any report can be disputed by other users.
  • Aggregate usage counts. A small number of in-app interaction events are recorded against a random installation identifier, not against your account. They contain no location data and no directly identifying information, and cannot be traced back to you once your account is gone.
  • Purchase and billing records, if you ever bought a subscription. These are held by Google Play, not by iMaps, and both we and Google are required to retain transaction records for tax and accounting purposes. Manage subscriptions in your Google Play account.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, for as long as the law requires.

Want a specific report removed? Because reports carry no account link, we cannot find yours from your email address alone. Email us with the approximate location, the report type and roughly when you submitted it, and we will do our best to identify and remove it.

Deleting only your data, without deleting your account

If you would rather keep your account but remove what is stored about you, you have two options:

  • On your device: Android Settings → Apps → iMaps → Storage → Clear data. This erases every preference and identifier iMaps holds locally, including the random identifiers, and generates fresh ones next time you open the app.
  • Sign out: Settings → Account → Sign out in the app clears your session and returns you to guest mode. Your account continues to exist until you request deletion.
