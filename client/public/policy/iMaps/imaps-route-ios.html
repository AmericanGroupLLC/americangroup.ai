Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

American Group LLC ("we", "our", or "us") operates the iMaps Route mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

Information We Collect

iMaps Route does not require users to create an account to use its core features.

The App does not intentionally collect, store, or sell your personal information. If you contact us for support, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

Location Information

If you choose to use location-based features, the App may request access to your device's location to provide maps, route guidance, or nearby place information. Your location is used only while providing these features and is not stored by us unless otherwise stated within the App.

How We Use Information

  • Display nearby places and destinations.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Improve the performance and reliability of the application.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared only when required by law or as necessary to provide map-related services through trusted third-party providers.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party mapping or location services to display maps, routes, and related information. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to help protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Children's Privacy

iMaps Route is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
