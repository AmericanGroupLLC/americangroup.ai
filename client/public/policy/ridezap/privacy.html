Accounts

The app does not require registration and does not create user accounts or profiles.

Device permissions

The app requests no camera, microphone, location, photo, contact or health permissions. Pick-up and drop-off places are typed, not taken from GPS.

Android permissions declared by the app: ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, INTERNET. Google’s ads and Firebase components add permissions that are granted without a prompt: WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, the advertising-ID permission (AD_ID), Android’s ad-services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) and Google Play’s install-referrer permission.

What the app stores on your device

The places you save (name, address and map coordinates), your saved trips, the fares you log, your rides log and your settings. They are kept in the app’s own storage on your phone and are never sent to us. Settings → Delete everything removes them, and uninstalling the app does too.

Advertising

RideZap is free and shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, at the bottom of a trip’s screen. There are no full-screen ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and a Privacy choices row in Settings lets you change it at any time. To serve and measure ads, Google receives information such as your IP address, device type, operating system and ad interactions. On Android this can include your device’s advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your phone’s settings; on iPhone the app never shows the tracking prompt, so the advertising identifier is not available. How Google uses this information is described in Google’s privacy policy. Your saved places, trips and fares are never sent with an ad request. We do not sell your personal information.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app sends diagnostics to Google Firebase, a service of Google LLC, so that problems can be found and fixed:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports, and reports of errors the app recovered from (for example an ad that failed to load): the stack trace, the app version, and the device model and operating-system version at the time.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — the events Analytics records automatically, such as first open, session start and time in the app. The app logs no custom events. On Android, Analytics may also collect the device’s advertising ID.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — app start time, screen rendering and similar timings.
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches configuration values when the app starts. RideZap does not use any of them: nothing in the app is switched on or off remotely.

These services identify an installation with a random Firebase installation ID and Analytics app-instance ID. They do not receive your saved places, trips or fares, and the app does not set a user ID. Google processes this data as described in Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s privacy policy.

Network

Besides the ads and diagnostics above, RideZap talks to two free map services built on OpenStreetMap, over HTTPS:

  • Place search — when you search for a place, the text you type is sent to Photon (photon.komoot.io, run by komoot), with the coordinates of your most recently saved place so nearby results come first.
  • Distance and drive time — the coordinates of a trip’s two ends are sent to the OSRM routing service at routing.openstreetmap.de (run by FOSSGIS e.V.).

Both services also see your IP address and the app’s name, version and our contact address, which they ask every app to send. Neither request carries an identifier for you.

When you tap Uber or Lyft, RideZap opens that company’s app or website with the trip’s pick-up and drop-off coordinates filled in (for Uber, also the places’ names and addresses). No referral or tracking parameter is added. From then on you are using that company’s service, under its privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you delete it with Settings → Delete everything or uninstall the app. We hold no copy. Data Google collects for ads and diagnostics is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
