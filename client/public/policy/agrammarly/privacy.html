Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-10-22 · Applies to AGrammarly for iOS and Android.

AGrammarly is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

1. What the app collects

DataWhyWhere it goes
Phrases and emoji you useMake the suggestions sound like youshared_preferences on your device. Never uploaded. Capped at the 50 most recent phrases.
Counters: suggestions used, keystrokes saved, emoji usedThe stats shown on HomeOn your device only.
The message you paste inMatch it against the pattern set to suggest repliesMatched in memory on the device. Not stored, not sent.

2. What the app does not collect

  • Your name, email address or any account - there is no sign-up
  • Your contacts, photos, files or location
  • The messages you paste in - they are matched on the device and not stored or sent
  • Anything you type, paste or have learned is never sent with an ad request or a crash report

3. Location

Not requested and not used.

4. Third parties

Advertising. The app shows ads served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may use the device advertising identifier to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and Settings → Privacy choices lets you change that answer. We do not sell your personal information.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, a report goes to Google Firebase Crashlytics so the bug can be fixed: the technical details of the crash, the device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier. It is not linked to you and never contains your messages, replies or learned phrases. There is no analytics.

5. Children

AGrammarly does not ask for a name, email address or account, and is not directed at children. The ads shown in it are served by Google under Google's ad-content policies.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

Settings has 'Clear everything learned', which deletes the learned style and the counters immediately. Uninstalling the app removes everything with it. We hold no copy of anything you type or the app learns, because none of it is sent. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

What the app has learned stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. We hold nothing; crash reports are kept by Google under Firebase's retention terms.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
