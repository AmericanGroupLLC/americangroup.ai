Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Tap "Continue as guest" on the login screen. Sign-in is optional and gates no features.

The compass or level shows nothing.

Those tools need a magnetometer and accelerometer. Emulators and some tablets lack them, and the app shows a graceful fallback rather than a fake reading.

The barometer says it is unavailable.

Many phones have no pressure sensor. Where it is absent the tool says so instead of estimating.

How accurate is the screen ruler?

It is calibrated to your device's reported screen density, which is accurate for most phones but is not a calibrated instrument. Use it for DIY and quick checks.

Does the noise meter record me?

No. Audio is sampled to compute a level and is never recorded, stored or transmitted.

How do I switch to imperial?

Settings - Units. The choice is remembered.
