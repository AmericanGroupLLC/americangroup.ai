iMeasure Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iMeasure ("iMeasure", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the iMeasure mobile application.

Privacy at a glance: iMeasure does not require users to create an account or sign in. The application is designed to provide measurement and utility features without requiring users to provide personal information for an account. The application does display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

1. Information We Collect

iMeasure does not require account creation or sign-in, and the current release does not provide a user account system requiring users to provide an email address, password, or other account credentials.

The application may request access to device features such as the camera, microphone, location, and sensors when required for specific measurement functionality. Information obtained through these features is processed on the device as described below.

2. Location Information

iMeasure may request access to your device's location for compass and true-north functionality.

Location information used by measurement features is processed on your device and is not transmitted to our servers for advertising, analytics, or account management.

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Some location-dependent features may not function if permission is denied.

3. Camera and Sensors

iMeasure may request access to the device camera for its measurement features, including the live camera-based measurement interface.

iMeasure may also use device sensors such as the accelerometer, magnetometer, gyroscope, and other supported sensors to provide measurement and orientation functionality.

Camera and sensor information used by these features is processed on the device. iMeasure does not upload camera images, photographs, or sensor readings to our servers.

4. Microphone

The Noise Meter feature may request microphone access to measure environmental sound levels.

Audio is processed on the device to calculate sound-level measurements. iMeasure does not record, store, or transmit microphone audio to our servers.

5. No Account or Authentication

iMeasure does not require users to create an account or sign in. The current release does not provide email/password authentication, Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or other user account functionality.

No account credentials, email addresses, or authentication identifiers are required or collected by the current release for account purposes.

6. Local Storage

iMeasure stores certain application preferences and settings locally on your device. These may include preferences such as theme, language, onboarding state, and other settings required for the application experience.

Measurement information processed locally by the application is not automatically transmitted to external servers unless a feature explicitly requires an online service.

7. Third-Party Services

The current release of iMeasure does not use third-party authentication services and does not require an online account or backend service for account management.

The application may use platform-provided device capabilities and APIs required to provide measurement functionality. Information obtained from these capabilities is processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

iMeasure does not use Supabase, Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or similar account authentication services in the current release.

8. Advertising

iMeasure displays banner and interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

To deliver and measure advertisements, Google may collect and process information such as your Android Advertising ID, IP address, general device information, and interactions with advertisements.

This information may be processed by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

Camera images, microphone audio, location information, and sensor readings used by iMeasure's measurement features are not shared by iMeasure with advertisers for advertising purposes.

iMeasure does not use camera images, microphone audio, location information, or sensor readings to create advertising profiles.

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through your Android device settings. The exact location of this setting may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

9. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The current release does not use third-party analytics or crash reporting services such as Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, PostHog, AppsFlyer, or OneSignal.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

iMeasure does not upload camera images, microphone audio, location information, or sensor readings to our servers for advertising, analytics, or account management.

Google AdMob may receive information required to serve and measure advertisements, including the Android Advertising ID, IP address, general device information, and ad interactions, as described in the Advertising section of this Privacy Policy.

Your measurement data is not provided by iMeasure to advertisers.

11. Data Security

Because iMeasure does not require an account and measurement information described in this policy is primarily processed locally on your device, most measurement data remains on your device.

Advertising-related network communications are handled by Google AdMob and other network services used by the application as applicable.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Your Choices

  • You can use iMeasure without creating an account or signing in.
  • You may decline camera, microphone, and location permissions.
  • You may revoke previously granted permissions through your device settings.
  • You may control or remove locally stored application data through your device settings or by uninstalling the application.
  • You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through your Android device settings.

13. Children's Privacy

iMeasure is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
