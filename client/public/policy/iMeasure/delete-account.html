iMeasure Account Deletion

This page explains how iMeasure users can request deletion of their account and associated account data.

Request Account Deletion

If you have an iMeasure account and would like your account and associated account data deleted, send a deletion request to:

contact@americangroup.ai

Please include the email address associated with your iMeasure account so that we can identify and process your request.

What Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is verified and processed, we will delete the account and associated personal information maintained for the iMeasure account, subject to any information that we are legally required to retain.

This may include account information such as:

  • Email address
  • Account/user identifier
  • Optional name or profile information associated with the account

Guest Users

iMeasure can be used without creating an account. Guest users do not need to submit an account deletion request because they do not have an iMeasure account.

Processing Time

We will review and process valid account deletion requests within a reasonable period after verifying the request.

Questions

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact:

contact@americangroup.ai
