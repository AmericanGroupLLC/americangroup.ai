Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-10-12 · Applies to MyHealth for iOS and Android.

MyHealth is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

2. What the app does not collect

  • Health data read from Apple Health or Google Fit - the app integrates with neither
  • Your contacts
  • Any identifier used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites - ads are non-tracking (see section 4)
  • Your health data for advertising or profiling - never, under any circumstance

3. Location

Not required. Where a run or hike records a route, that stays on your device.

4. Third parties

Optional sign-in is handled by Supabase, which sees your email address if you use it. No health entry is sent to any third party.

Advertising. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner on the main screens and, occasionally, a full-screen ad between screens (at most one every few minutes). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under “Privacy choices” in the app. Ads are non-tracking: we do not link ad data to your identity or use it to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. To serve and measure ads, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier and IP address, as described in Google’s privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information. Health data you enter in the app is never shared with Google AdMob or any other ad network and is never used for advertising, and ads are not personalised from it.

Crash reporting and analytics. Crash reporting and analytics are off unless you turn them on. Only after you accept the consent screen and switch on “Send crash reports” or “Share anonymous usage analytics” in Settings (both are off by default) does the app send, respectively, crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics (the error, the device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier) or anonymous usage statistics and performance measurements through Google Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring. You can switch either off again at any time. This data is not linked to your name or account, we do not use it for advertising, and Google processes it under Firebase’s privacy terms.

5. Children

Not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

Delete an entry in the app to remove it. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes your entire local record. If you signed in, email us to have that account and its synced data deleted. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

Your entries stay on your device until you delete them. Scanned images are discarded after the on-device extraction. Any sync traffic uses HTTPS.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
