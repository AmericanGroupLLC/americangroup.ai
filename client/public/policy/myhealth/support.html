Support

Questions and bug reports for MyHealth go to one place, and a person reads them. We cannot answer questions about your health - please speak to a qualified clinician.

Contact us

Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Tap "Continue as guest" on the login screen. Sign-in only syncs your data across devices.

Does it read Apple Health or Google Fit?

No. MyHealth integrates with neither. Everything in the app is what you logged yourself.

Why do some Care screens look like sample data?

Because they are, and they say so on screen. The doctor finder, MyChart/FHIR view and the insurance, lab and prescription extractions demonstrate capabilities that need clinical integrations this build does not have.

Is my health data uploaded?

Not unless you choose to sign in for sync. By default everything stays on your device, and it is never used for advertising.

Can MyHealth tell me if something is wrong?

No. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose anything, and nothing in it is medical advice. Speak to a clinician.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual entries in the app, or clear app data to remove everything. If you signed in, email us to delete the account.
