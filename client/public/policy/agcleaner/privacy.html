Accounts

The app does not require registration and does not create user accounts or profiles.

Device permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you use the related feature. You can decline either one and still use the rest of the app.

  • Contacts — AGCleaner reads your contacts to find cards that describe the same person, including cards saved in different accounts (iCloud, Google, Exchange or on the phone). It changes your contacts only when you tap Merge or Restore. Before a merge changes anything, the original cards are saved as a vCard file inside the app on your device; if that save fails, nothing is changed. A merge updates the card you keep and deletes the others. Restore puts the originals back. Your phone then syncs those changes to the account each card belongs to, as it does for any edit you make. Contacts are never uploaded by the app.
  • Photo library — AGCleaner reads your photo library to find byte-for-byte identical copies. Nothing is uploaded, and copies are deleted only when you confirm.

Android permissions declared by the app: READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and earlier) and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 10 and earlier). Google’s ads and Firebase components add permissions that are granted without a prompt: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS and Google Play’s install-referrer permission. The advertising-ID permission (AD_ID) and the ad-services ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION permissions are removed.

What the app stores on your device

Your scan history, the groups you marked “Not the same person”, and a restore point for each merge: a vCard file with the original cards, with the date and the names of the cards it holds. These files are kept in the app’s own storage on your device and are never sent to us. You can share a restore point yourself from the Restore points screen, which opens your phone’s share sheet, and you can delete a restore point there. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

AGCleaner is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. There is a banner at the bottom of the Home, Contacts and Photos screens (on Contacts and Photos it is hidden while a scan is running) and, occasionally, a full-screen ad when you return to the Home screen (at most one every three minutes). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. The app never shows the iOS tracking prompt and removes the advertising-ID permission on Android, so ads are served without your device’s advertising identifier. To serve and measure ads, Google receives information such as your IP address, device type and operating system, as described in Google’s privacy policy. Your contacts and photos are never sent with an ad request. We do not sell your personal information.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app sends diagnostics to Google Firebase, a service of Google LLC, so that problems can be found and fixed. It uses these Firebase products:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error data: the stack trace of a crash, or of an error the app recovered from (for example an ad that failed to load), the app version, and the device model and operating-system version at the time.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — product interaction: the events Analytics records automatically, such as first open, session start and time in the app. The app logs no custom events.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — performance data: app start time, screen rendering and similar timings.
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches configuration values when the app starts. AGCleaner does not use any of them: nothing in the app is switched on or off remotely.

These services identify an installation with a random Firebase installation ID and Analytics app-instance ID. They do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, file names or file contents, and the app does not set a user ID. The data is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising, and is not used to track you across apps or websites. On Android, Firebase Analytics’ collection of the advertising ID and its ad-personalisation signals are switched off; on iPhone the advertising identifier is not available to it, because the app never asks for tracking permission. Google processes this data as described in Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s privacy policy.

Network

Finding duplicates, hashing, merging and restore points all run on your device. No contact, photo or file content is ever sent anywhere by the app. The only network traffic of the app itself is the Firebase diagnostics and the Google AdMob ad requests described above, sent over HTTPS. On iPhone, if a photo is kept only in iCloud, iOS downloads it from your iCloud Photos so it can be compared; that transfer is between your phone and Apple.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores, including restore points, stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Contacts that you merged or restored stay in your address book, because they are your contacts and not app data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
