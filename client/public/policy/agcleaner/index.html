Accounts

The app does not require registration and does not create user accounts or profiles.

Device permissions

The app asks for the following permissions only when you use the related feature, and each request explains its purpose with the text below. You can decline any of them and keep using the rest of the app.

  • NSContactsUsageDescription — AGCleaner reads your contacts to find entries that describe the same person. It never edits, deletes or uploads a contact.
  • Photo library — AGCleaner reads your photo library to find byte-for-byte identical copies. Nothing is uploaded, and copies are only deleted when you confirm it.

Android permissions declared: READ_CONTACTS, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.

Advertising

The app does not show advertisements and does not include an advertising SDK.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app sends anonymous diagnostics to Google Firebase, a service of Google LLC, so that problems can be found and fixed. It uses these Firebase products:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash data: the stack trace, the app version, and the device model and operating-system version at the time of a crash.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — product interaction: the events Analytics records automatically, such as first open, session start and time in the app. The app logs no custom events.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — performance data: app start time, screen rendering and similar timings.
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches configuration values for the app.

These services identify an installation with a random Firebase installation ID and Analytics app-instance ID. They do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, file names or file contents, and the app does not set a user ID. The data is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising, and is not used to track you across apps or websites. The app does not collect the device advertising ID. Crash, analytics and performance data are collected only by store (release) builds. Google processes this data as described in Firebase's privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

Network

Duplicate detection, hashing and deletion run entirely on your device; no photo, contact or file content is ever sent anywhere. The only network traffic is the Firebase diagnostics described above, sent over HTTPS. The Firebase libraries add the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions to the Android app for this.

Data retention and deletion

Data the app stores lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
