Support

Questions and bug reports for Fashion Factory go to one place, and a person reads them.

Contact us

Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

A price looks wrong.

Prices change constantly and we show what we last saw. Always confirm at the retailer's checkout. If a price is persistently wrong, email us the product and the store.

Why is a store missing from the comparison?

We can only show stores our price sources cover, and stock varies by region. Missing stores are gaps in coverage, not a claim that the product is unavailable there.

How do drop alerts work?

Save a product and we watch its price. You get a notification when it falls. Alerts need notification permission.

Do you earn money from links?

Some retailer links are affiliate links and may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects the ranking in the compare table.

Where did my saved items go?

They are stored on your device, so clearing app data or uninstalling removes them.
