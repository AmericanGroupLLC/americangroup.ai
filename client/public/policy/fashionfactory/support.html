Support

Fashion Factory keeps a record of your online clothing and shoe orders: the size you ordered, whether you kept it or sent it back and why, and the date it has to go back by. Questions and bug reports go to one place, and a person reads them.

Contact us

Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings → About.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Everything you enter is stored on your phone.

How is “Your size” at a brand worked out?

Only from orders you logged. Type a brand under Find my size on the home screen (and optionally pick a category). The answer is the size you most recently kept at that brand, checked against anything you returned there as too small, too big, too long or too short, and it lists the orders it is based on. If what you kept and what you sent back disagree, it says Mixed results rather than guessing. If you have no history at that brand, it says so, and shows what you kept in other categories or at other brands, labelled as such. The app has no brand size charts and never converts sizes.

An order is still under “Out for trial”.

Orders stay there until you record what happened. Tap the order, then Kept, returned or exchanged?. Returns “didn't like it” and “faulty” are kept for your records but do not change your size answer, because they say nothing about fit. An exchange logs the new size as its own order, out for trial.

I didn't get a return reminder.

Reminders need notification permission. The app asks the first time you save an order with a return-by date. Open Settings → Return reminders to see their state; if they are off, the button there turns them on or opens your phone's notification settings. When they are on, each order with a return-by date gets one reminder 3 days before the last day (or 1 day before, if you choose that in Settings) and one on the last day, at 9 am, or around 9 am where the phone does not allow exact timing. Orders saved with No returns get no reminders, and marking an order kept, returned or exchanged, or deleting it, cancels its reminders. The countdown on the home screen works either way.

Does the app read the product page or track prices?

No. A link you paste is stored with the order, and the store name is filled in from the web address. The app never downloads the page; Open on an order hands the link to your browser. The price you paid is optional and only feeds the “Returned this year” total on the home screen.

What sizes can I enter?

Letters (S, M, XL…), numbers, waist and length (W32 L32), shoe sizes with their system (US men's, US women's, UK, EU or cm), One size, or anything else, which is kept exactly as you typed it.

How do I share my sizes or get my orders out?

Settings → Share my sizes lets you tick the sizes you kept and send them as text, for example to someone buying you a gift. Settings → Export orders (CSV) creates a spreadsheet file of every order. Both go through your phone's share sheet, so you choose where they go.

Can I move my orders to a new phone?

There is no sync and no import: the CSV export is a copy for a spreadsheet, and the app cannot read it back in.

Why are there ads?

The app is free and shows a Google AdMob banner at the end of the home screen and of a brand page. There are no ads on the order form or in Settings, and no full-screen ads. Where Google's consent form applies, Settings → Privacy choices reopens it.

What happened to price comparison and saved products?

Fashion Factory 3.0 replaced the earlier price-comparison app, which never had a live price source. Products saved in version 2 are not carried over.
