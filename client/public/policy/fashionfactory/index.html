Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-08-28 · Applies to Fashion Factory for iOS and Android.

Fashion Factory is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

1. What the app collects

DataWhyWhere it goes
Products you save and searches you runShow your saved list and power drop alertsStays on your device
Product queriesFetch current prices from retailer sourcesSent to the price sources to answer your query; not tied to an identifier for you
Notification preferencesDeliver drop alertsStays on your device

2. What the app does not collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your device location
  • Your contacts, photos, or camera
  • Any advertising or cross-app tracking identifier

3. Location

Not requested and not used.

4. Third parties

Prices come from retailer and price-aggregation sources, which see the request and its originating IP. Where a link out to a retailer is an affiliate link, a purchase may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you; this never changes the ranking shown in the compare table.

5. Children

Not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

There is no account, so nothing exists server-side to delete. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes your saved list, searches and settings. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

Saved products and search history stay on your device until you clear them. Network requests use HTTPS.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
