Frequently asked

Is this an official USCIS app?

No. It is an independent app, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by USCIS, the Department of State, or any government agency.

Can you tell me what will happen with my case?

No. We are not attorneys and cannot give legal advice. The app shows you official data and explains what stages mean; decisions about your case need a qualified immigration attorney.

Do I need an account?

No. Tap "Continue as guest". Sign-in is optional and syncs your case list across devices if you want it.

My status has not changed in months. Is the app broken?

Usually not - many cases sit unchanged for long periods. Compare against Processing Times to see whether yours is outside the posted range.

Where does the data come from?

Public USCIS and Department of State sources. Government Sources links to the official page behind every figure so you can verify it.

Are my receipt numbers uploaded anywhere?

A receipt number is sent to the public USCIS status endpoint to look it up - that is how any status check works. It is stored on your device, not on our servers, unless you opt into sign-in and sync.
