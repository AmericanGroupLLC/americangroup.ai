Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

American Group LLC ("we", "our", or "us") operates USCIS Case Lookup. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

Overview

USCIS Case Lookup helps users view publicly available USCIS case status information by entering a valid USCIS receipt number. The application is intended solely as a convenient interface for accessing publicly available case status information.

Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account or register to use the application.

The application does not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information.

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

How Information Is Used

Any information voluntarily provided through support communications is used only to:

  • Respond to your questions.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve the quality of the application.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except where required by applicable law.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Children's Privacy

USCIS Case Lookup is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The application may communicate with publicly available USCIS services solely to retrieve the case status requested by the user. We do not control the privacy practices of external services or websites.

Disclaimer

USCIS Case Lookup is an independent application developed by American Group LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, or any other government agency. All case information displayed by the application is obtained from publicly available USCIS resources.
