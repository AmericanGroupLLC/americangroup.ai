1. About This Application

Case Tracker for USCIS is a local-first personal organizational tool that allows users to monitor publicly available status updates for U.S. immigration cases, view processing time estimates, read agency news feeds, and check visa bulletins.

The app displays public information retrieved directly from official government agency web services. The app does not alter, create, or influence official government records.

3. Data Collection and Usage

We believe in strict data privacy and a local-first architecture:

  • Local Case Storage: Case receipt numbers and case details added to the app are stored locally on your device's private storage. They are never sold or shared with advertisers and are never sent to advertising services.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or provide personal identifiers such as your name, phone number, Social Security number (SSN), or email address to use the tracking functions.
  • Network Requests: When looking up a case status or processing time, the app transmits the receipt number or form type over a secure HTTPS connection to fetch official status data.
  • Advertising: This app displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your Android Advertising ID, IP address, general device information, and interactions with ads. This information is processed by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services . Your USCIS receipt numbers and case details are never shared with advertisers or used for ad targeting.
  • No Push Notification Tokens: Status updates and alerts are generated locally on your device during refresh cycles. No remote push notification tokens are tracked or transmitted.

4. App Permissions

The app requests minimal Android permissions. Advertising services may also use Android's advertising identifier as described in the Advertising section above.

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to send HTTPS requests to query public government status endpoints and display updates.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your tracked case numbers are stored locally on your mobile device:

  • You can delete any tracked case number individually from inside the app at any time.
  • Clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all saved case numbers and local preferences from your device.
