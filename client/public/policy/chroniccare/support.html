Questions and bug reports go to one place. We cannot answer questions about your health or your treatment - please speak to your clinician.

Contact us

Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

Frequently asked

Does it connect to Apple Health or Google Fit?

No. The app integrates with neither. Everything in it is what you logged yourself.

Can the app tell me if my readings are dangerous?

No. It is not a medical device and does not interpret or monitor your condition. If you are worried about a reading, contact your clinician; if it is an emergency, contact emergency services.

Is my health data used for advertising?

No. Never. The app carries no advertising SDK and reads no advertising identifier.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us and we will action it within 30 days. Individual entries can be deleted in the app at any time.

Will it remind me to take my medication?

Yes, if you add the medication and allow notifications. Reminders are a convenience, not a safety system - do not rely on the app alone for a critical dose.

Which conditions is it built around?

Diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, asthma, and quitting smoking.
