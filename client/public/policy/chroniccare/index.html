Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-08-28 · Applies to Chronic Care for iOS and Android.

Chronic Care is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

1. What the app collects

DataWhyWhere it goes
Symptoms, vitals and notes you logBuild your record and your trendsStored against your account on our backend so it survives a device change
Medications and reminder timesRemind you and keep your listStored against your account
Conditions you select in ProfileTune what the app asks you to logStored against your account
Email address or Apple/Google identifierSign you inHeld by our authentication provider

2. What the app does not collect

  • Health data read from Apple Health or Google Fit - the app integrates with neither
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos or microphone
  • Any advertising or cross-app tracking identifier
  • Your health data for advertising or profiling - never

3. Location

Not requested and not used.

4. Third parties

Authentication and storage run on Supabase. Sign in with Apple and Google are handled by Apple and Google respectively. No health entry is sent to an advertiser or data broker.

5. Children

Not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

Delete an entry in the app to remove it. To delete your account and everything in it, email us and we will action it within 30 days. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

Your entries persist while your account exists. All traffic uses HTTPS. Health data is treated as sensitive and is never used for advertising.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
