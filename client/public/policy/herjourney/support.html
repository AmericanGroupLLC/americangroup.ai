Frequently asked

Is HerJourney medical advice?

No. It is an education and self-tracking tool. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose anything, and it is not contraception. Talk to a clinician about any decision concerning your health.

Are the fertile window and due date predictions?

They are calculations from the dates you enter, using standard cycle arithmetic. They are not clinical predictions and they cannot account for your individual circumstances.

Where is my data stored?

In a database on your device. Nothing you log is uploaded, and there is no analytics SDK in the app.

Do I need an account?

No. Onboarding can be skipped and every feature works without signing in. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional and authenticates only - it does not upload your logs, and cross-device sync is not enabled in this version.

Do I have to connect Apple Health or Health Connect?

No. It is optional. Granting access lets the app read metrics your phone or watch already recorded and write menstrual flow, water and weight back. Declining leaves every other feature working.

Why does the Assistant say its answer is offline?

Because this build ships without an AI key, so the Assistant answers from a built-in set of general responses rather than generating one. The app labels those answers so you always know which you are reading.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the on-device database with it. If you signed in and want the account removed, email us and we will action it within 30 days.
