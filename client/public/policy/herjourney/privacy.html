Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-08-28 · Applies to HerJourney for iOS and Android.

HerJourney is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

1. What the app collects

2. What the app does not collect

  • Your contacts, calendar or files
  • Any advertising or cross-app tracking identifier - there is no advertising SDK in the app
  • Your location
  • Your health data for advertising, profiling or sale - never, under any circumstance

3. Location

Not requested and not used.

4. Third parties

Optional sign-in is handled by Supabase, which sees the Apple or Google account identifier you sign in with, and nothing else - no health entry is sent with it. Apple Health and Health Connect are on-device platform services and receive only the menstrual flow, water and weight values the app writes back, with your permission. A build configured with an AI key would send the question you type and the profile context described above to that AI provider; the build published to the stores has no AI key and answers offline instead. There is no advertising network and no analytics provider.

5. Children

HerJourney is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. It covers reproductive and sexual health, which is why it is rated 12+.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

Delete an entry in the app to remove it. Uninstalling HerJourney deletes the on-device database and everything in it. If you signed in and want the account itself removed, email us and we will action it within 30 days. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

Your entries stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Images you pick for the report screen are read to display them and are not copied off the device. Because nothing is uploaded, we hold no copy of your health data to retain.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
