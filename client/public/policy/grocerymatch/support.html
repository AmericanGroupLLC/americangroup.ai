Support

Questions and bug reports for GroceryMatch go to one place, and a person reads them.

Contact us

Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Tap "Continue as Guest (Demo)". Sign-in only syncs your lists across devices.

A price does not match my store.

Grocery prices are set per store and per region and change often. We show what we last saw for your area; confirm at the register.

Why does the app want my location?

Only to find stores and restaurants near you. Decline it and enter a postal code instead.

Why does it want the camera?

Only for receipt and barcode scanning, which is optional. Images are processed to extract items and are not kept.

Is Split Trip worth it?

That is exactly what it calculates - it compares the saving against the cost of a second stop and tells you when it is not worth it.

Do you take a cut of my order?

Some outbound ordering links are affiliate links and may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes the ranking we show.
