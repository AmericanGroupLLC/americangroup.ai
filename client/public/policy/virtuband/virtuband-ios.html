VirtuBand Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

American Group LLC ("we", "our", or "us") operates the VirtuBand mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use VirtuBand on iOS and other supported platforms.

Information We Collect

VirtuBand is designed to minimize data collection. Most features can be used without creating an account.

If you choose to sign in, we may collect:

  • Email address used for account authentication
  • Account identifiers required to provide account services

How We Use Information

Information collected through VirtuBand is used only to:

  • Provide account functionality
  • Maintain and improve app features
  • Provide user support

Music Practice Data

Practice information, recordings, and learning activity created inside VirtuBand are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them using your device's available sharing features.

Microphone Access

VirtuBand may request microphone permission for features such as recording and audio practice tools. Microphone data is processed only for the requested feature and is not uploaded or sold to third parties.

Third-Party Services

VirtuBand may use trusted services required for account authentication and app functionality. These services process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. Account-related communication is transmitted using secure connections.

Data Retention and Deletion

You may request deletion of your account-related information by contacting us.

Email:
contact@americangroup.ai

Children's Privacy

VirtuBand does not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

Your Privacy Choices

You may use VirtuBand without signing in. You can also stop using the application or contact us regarding your personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
