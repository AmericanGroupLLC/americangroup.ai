Delete Your VirtuBand Account

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

VirtuBand allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data. This page explains how to request account deletion and what information is removed.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like your VirtuBand account permanently deleted, please send an email to:

contact@americangroup.ai

Please include:

  • Your registered email address.
  • Your VirtuBand username (if applicable).
  • The subject line: Delete My VirtuBand Account.

What Happens After Your Request

Once your identity is verified, we will process your request and permanently delete your account within a reasonable period, unless we are required by law to retain certain information.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your account information.
  • Your profile information.
  • Your authentication information associated with your VirtuBand account.
  • Your app preferences stored with your account.
  • Any cloud-stored user content associated with your account, where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or regulatory compliance. Any retained information will only be kept for the period required by applicable law.

Guest Users

If you use VirtuBand without creating an account (Guest Mode), no online account exists to delete. You can remove locally stored app data at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the app's storage from your device settings.

Need Help?

If you have questions about your account or this deletion process, please contact us at:

contact@americangroup.ai
