Support

Questions and bug reports for VirtuBand go to one place, and a person reads them.

Contact us

Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Tap "Continue as Guest". Sign-in is optional and gates no features; you can add it later from Settings - Account.

I hear no sound.

Audio needs a physical device - simulators often render the interface silently. Also check the device is not muted and that microphone permission was granted if you are recording.

Why does the app want the microphone?

Only to record your performance. Audio is saved to your device and is not uploaded.

Are my recordings uploaded?

No. Recordings stay on your device. Only an AI Compose prompt you submit leaves the device.

Where did my projects go?

Projects are stored locally, so clearing app data or uninstalling removes them. Sign in if you want them synced across devices.

What does Premium add?

Premium is an optional upgrade; the free app is fully usable without it. The paywall in Settings lists what is included.

Report a privacy concern or request deletion

See the Privacy Policy, or email contact@americangroup.ai with the subject “Data request”.
