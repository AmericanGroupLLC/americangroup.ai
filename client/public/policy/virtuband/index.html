Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

The short version. VirtuBand is designed as an offline-first virtual music studio. Your instruments, song sections and recordings are stored on your device. You can use the core features without creating an account. VirtuBand also displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the VirtuBand mobile application (“VirtuBand”, “the app”, “we”) handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the VirtuBand app published under the package name com.americangroupllc.virtuband.

1. Using VirtuBand without an account

Signing in is optional. You can tap Continue as Guest and use the core features without providing an email address or other account information.

If you later choose to sign in, you can do so from Settings → Account. Signing out returns you to guest mode and does not prevent you from continuing to use the app.

2. Information stored on your device

The following information may be saved in local storage on your device. This information is not transmitted to us unless a feature explicitly requires an external service.

What Why
Language preference To show the app in your chosen language
Onboarding and guest status So you are not repeatedly shown onboarding or the sign-in screen
Song sections (name, tempo, key, bars) To restore your arrangement when you reopen the app
Recording metadata (title, duration, date, tempo, genre) To list and manage your saved takes
Audio recordings Saved as files in the app's private storage so you can play them back
Signed-in email address (only if you sign in) To restore and maintain your account session

Uninstalling the app removes locally stored application data. You can also delete individual recordings from the Recordings screen.

3. Microphone

VirtuBand requests the RECORD_AUDIO permission only when you start a recording.

Microphone audio is used to capture recordings that you explicitly start. Recordings are written to your device's private app storage and are not uploaded by VirtuBand.

If you decline microphone permission, other features continue to work; you simply cannot record audio.

4. Account and authentication

If you choose to create or use an account, authentication is handled by Supabase . Supabase may process your email address and authentication credentials on our behalf in order to verify your identity and maintain your session.

We do not use your email address for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell or rent your email address.

Account functionality requires an internet connection.

5. Analytics

VirtuBand does not integrate a third-party analytics SDK for tracking individual users.

The application may record limited anonymous, aggregate interaction information during the current session, such as whether playback was started or a language was changed. These interaction counts do not contain personal identifiers and do not contain audio recordings.

6. Advertising

VirtuBand displays banner and/or interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

To deliver, measure, and improve advertising, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and interactions with advertisements.

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

VirtuBand does not provide your saved song arrangements, audio recordings, microphone recordings, or other locally stored music content to advertisers.

VirtuBand does not use your microphone recordings or saved music content to create advertising profiles.

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through your Android device settings. The exact location of this setting may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not upload your recordings, arrangements, or microphone audio to our servers for advertising purposes.
  • We do not track your physical location. The app does not request location permission.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, calendar, SMS, or call logs for the purpose of providing the app.
  • We do not use your saved recordings or song arrangements for advertising targeting.
  • We do not use third-party social-media SDKs.

8. Children's privacy

VirtuBand is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at contact@americangroup.ai and we will review the request.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when required for specific features of the application.

For example, if you use account functionality, authentication-related information may be processed by Supabase. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google AdMob to provide and measure advertisements.

Your locally stored recordings, song arrangements, and microphone audio are not provided by VirtuBand to advertisers.

10. Your rights and choices

Because much of VirtuBand's content is stored locally, you have direct control over much of your application data.

  • You can use the core app features without creating an account.
  • You can delete recordings from within the app.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove locally stored application data.
  • You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through Android device settings.
  • If you have an account, you may contact us regarding access, correction, or deletion of account information.

11. Data retention

On-device application data is retained until you delete it, clear the application data, or uninstall the app.

Account information, if you create an account, may be retained while your account remains active or until you request deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

12. Security

Account-related communications use encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS where supported.

Your recordings remain in the app's private storage area and are not uploaded by VirtuBand.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. International transfers

If you use account functionality, authentication-related information may be processed by our service providers on servers located outside your country.

Advertising-related information may also be processed by Google and its service providers in accordance with Google's applicable privacy practices and policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date on this page.
