Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Verba states "No Account Needed" and every feature works on first launch.

Why does iOS ask about tracking?

Verba shows ads through Google AdMob. The prompt asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier for ad relevance. Decline it and everything still works.

Is my audio recorded or kept?

Audio is sent for recognition and translation and is not retained by us afterwards. We do not store recordings of your conversations.

The translation is wrong.

Automated translation struggles with accents, background noise, idiom and specialist vocabulary. For anything medical, legal, financial or safety-related, use a qualified human interpreter.

How do the flashcards decide what to show?

Spaced repetition - a word comes back just before you would have forgotten it, so you spend time on what you do not know.

How do I remove ads?

Me - Premium. The upgrade removes ads; the free app is otherwise fully usable.
