Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-08-28 · Applies to Verba for iOS and Android.

Verba is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

1. What the app collects

DataWhyWhere it goes
Audio you record for translationRecognise and translate speechSent to the translation service for processing; not retained by us afterwards
Text you type to translateReturn the translationSent to the translation service for processing
Phrasebook entries and flashcard progressYour saved words and review scheduleStays on your device
Advertising identifier, with consentShow and measure ads via Google AdMobHandled by Google AdMob under its own policy; declining the ATT prompt withholds it

2. What the app does not collect

  • Your name, email or phone number - no account is required
  • Your contacts, photos or location
  • A recording of your conversations - audio is processed and not stored by us

3. Location

Not requested and not used.

4. Third parties

Speech recognition and translation run server-side. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which operates under its own privacy policy and receives the advertising identifier when you consent. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google.

5. Children

Not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their data. Because the app serves ads, it is not suitable for a children's category.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

Delete a phrasebook entry or reset flashcard progress in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes everything local. There is no account holding your data. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

Phrasebook and flashcard data stay on your device until you clear them. Audio and text submitted for translation are processed and not retained by us. All traffic uses HTTPS.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
