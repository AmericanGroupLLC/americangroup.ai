Privacy Policy for CarDeal

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

Welcome to CarDeal, a mobile application owned and operated by AmericanGroupLLC. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our car buying, selling, and valuation platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless car trading experience, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile details.
  • Vehicle Details: Photos, specifications, and pricing info you upload to create car listings.
  • Location Data: Approximate location to help you find nearby car deals.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to facilitate vehicle buying and selling transactions, verify listings, calculate car valuations, and communicate essential updates about your account.

3. Data Security

AmericanGroupLLC uses industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner on the main screens and, occasionally, a full-screen ad between screens (at most one every few minutes). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under “Privacy choices” in the app. Ads are non-tracking: we do not link ad data to your identity or use it to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. To serve and measure ads, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier and IP address, as described in Google’s privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Crash Reporting

To find and fix problems, the app sends crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics (the error, the device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier). It does not send usage analytics. This data is not linked to your name or account, we do not use it for advertising, and Google processes it under Firebase’s privacy terms.
