Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-10-12 · Applies to DriftDate for iOS and Android.

DriftDate is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

2. What the app does not collect

  • Your precise coordinates - they are blurred before use
  • Your contacts
  • Your payment details - handled by Apple or Google
  • Any identifier used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites - ads are non-tracking (see section 4)

3. Location

Coarsened by a LocationFuzzer before it is used or stored. Raw coordinates are never exposed to another user, and no precise position is shown to anyone.

4. Third parties

Authentication, storage and messaging run on Supabase. Reply suggestions are generated by a model provider that receives the message context needed to produce a suggestion. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google.

Advertising. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner on the main screens and, occasionally, a full-screen ad between screens (at most one every few minutes). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under “Privacy choices” in the app. Ads are non-tracking: we do not link ad data to your identity or use it to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. To serve and measure ads, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier and IP address, as described in Google’s privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

Crash reporting and analytics. To find and fix problems, the app sends crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics (the error, the device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier), and anonymous usage statistics and performance measurements through Google Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring. This data is not linked to your name or account, we do not use it for advertising, and Google processes it under Firebase’s privacy terms.

5. Children

DriftDate is strictly for adults and is rated 17+. We do not knowingly allow or collect data from anyone under 18, and accounts found to belong to minors are removed.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

Delete your account from Settings and your profile, photos and messages are removed. You can also email us to request deletion, and we act within 30 days. Blocks and abuse reports may be retained after deletion where needed to keep other users safe. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

Your profile and messages persist while your account exists. Reports and safety records may be retained longer where required to protect other users. All traffic uses HTTPS.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
