Support

Questions, bug reports and safety concerns for DriftDate go to one place, and a person reads them. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Contact us

Email contact@americangroup.ai. We reply within one business day. To help us reproduce a problem, please include your device model, OS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

Frequently asked

How do I report someone?

Open their profile and use Report, or go to the Safety Centre. Reports are reviewed by a person.

How do I block someone?

From their profile, at any time. Blocks take effect immediately and appear in Blocked Users.

Can anyone see exactly where I am?

No. Your location is deliberately blurred before it is used, and raw coordinates are never exposed to another user.

Why can I only see people in my ZIP code?

That is the design - discovery starts local and widens when you choose. Use the layer control to move to county, state or everywhere.

How do I delete my account?

Settings - Delete account. Your profile, photos and messages are removed. Safety reports may be retained to protect other users.

I did not get my sign-in code.

Codes arrive by SMS and can be delayed by your carrier. Request a new one after the timer, and check the number you entered.
